## K-0001 — EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED (architectural_fact)

**Claim.** In Gemma 4 E2B (google/gemma-4-E2B-it rev 3e22461, transformers 5.20.2), large sampled perturbations (Gaussian, sd 50) of the residual stream at all non-final positions entering layers 15, 16, 25 or 34 left final-position logits exactly unchanged, while the same perturbations entering layers 5, 13 or 14 changed them (max |Δlogit| 17.7-36.4). Together with the implementation (layers >= 15 read only K/V stored by layers 13/14), this indicates non-final positions after layer 14 are causally inert for final-position outputs.

**Scope.** Gemma 4 E2B text decoder, inputs shorter than the 512-token sliding window, no KV cache, fp32 compute; implementation in transformers 5.20.2

**Conditions.** 2 prompts (10 and 15 tokens); noise sd 50 per coordinate; Runner hooks at resid_pre

**Verification.** Deterministic perturbation test + source-code reading; preregistered as P-ARCH-1 in experiments/E001_arch/PREREG.md

**Primary sources.** LIT03-004 (transformers: modeling_gemma4.py (Gemma4 decoder implementation)); LIT03-001 (Gemma 4 Technical Report)

- supports: `experiments/E001_arch/result.json` — Gaussian noise (sd 50) on all non-final positions: max|Δlogit| = 0.0 at layers 15/16/25/34 vs 17.7-36.4 at layers 5/13/14; 2 prompts; identical in two separate runs
- supports: `LIT03-004` — code: layers >= num_layers - num_kv_shared_layers (=15) take K/V from shared_kv_states stored by the last non-shared layer of the same type (13 sliding, 14 full)

**Artifacts.** `experiments/E001_arch/PREREG.md`, `experiments/E001_arch/result.json`, `experiments/E001_arch/run.py`, `experiments/lib/runner.py`

## K-0008 — EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED (experimental_observation)

**Scope.** single sequence, batch 1, CPU (AVX-512F, no bf16 GEMM), fp32 compute with bf16 weights, final-position output only (first generated token); decode with KV cache, batching and GPU not measured; energy not measurable in this sandbox

**Conditions.** Runner.forward(logits='last') vs Runner.forward(last_only_from=15, allow_long=True); 1 warm-up + 3 interleaved repeats; shared host (timing noise: FULL T=1024 runs 61-85 s)

**Verification.** Preregistered E007 (PREREG.md); transformers source inspected (Gemma4TextModel loops all layers; logits_to_keep only slices lm_head)
